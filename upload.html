    Common questions

    What happens when someone I don't know calls?

    The call connects as normal, but they'll first hear a brief message letting them know the line is protected. While you're talking, Home Call Guard monitors the conversation for signs of a scam, and can end the call if it detects serious scam risk.

    Will calls from my family and friends be affected?

    No. Trusted contacts ring straight through, and their calls aren't monitored.

    Is my home phone number changing?

    No — you keep your existing number.

    What if I need help?

    Contact us any time at support@homecallguard.co.uk and we'll be glad to help.
