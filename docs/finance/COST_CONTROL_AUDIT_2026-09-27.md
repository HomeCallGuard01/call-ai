# Cost-control audit: remaining ways HCG could incur uncontrolled recurring provider cost

Audited 2026-09-27 against `origin/main` eb43368, the live Twilio account and staging, all read-only. Lifecycle items owned by the number-lifecycle (P0) work are listed for completeness, not changed here.

| # | Exposure | Evidence | Severity | Owner / action |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Staging and local development buy real numbers on the production Twilio account** | 7 of 19 billed numbers belong to staging households (voice URL points at a dev ngrok tunnel); 1 Twilio account, no sub-accounts. Staging Stripe test subscriptions never lapse, so those numbers never enter a release lifecycle. £6.08/month today and growing with every staging signup. | HIGH (recurring, invisible) | **DECISION REQUIRED:** Twilio sub-account (or separate account) for staging/dev, with its own balance cap, and release of today's staging numbers via the lifecycle. |
| 2 | **Apple sandbox/TestFlight purchases grant real memberships and buy real numbers** | `revenuecatWebhook.js` never reads the event's `environment`; two households received production numbers from Apple sandbox purchases. | MEDIUM | **DECISION REQUIRED:** App Review needs sandbox purchases to work, so don't block them. Classify them (e.g. `sandbox` source), give them a bounded expiry, and route their numbers through the release lifecycle. |
| 3 | **No provider-side spend alarm** | Twilio usage triggers configured: **0**. Nothing alerts on a runaway day (loop, flood, bug) except the HCG app itself. | HIGH | Configure Twilio usage triggers (daily total price; numbers count), a provider-side control independent of HCG code. Needs account change approval. |
| 4 | **Quarantined numbers can be held indefinitely** | 2 quarantined numbers; release needs a manual confirmation that has never happened. | MEDIUM | P0 lifecycle (45/90-day escalation approved; implementation in progress). |
| 5 | **Numbers outside any lifecycle** | QA and legacy-dev households hold numbers with no current entitlement and no pending release; complimentary memberships expiring by date don't start release on `main`. | MEDIUM | P0 lifecycle sweep (`050_number_lifecycle_sweep_evidence`, applied to staging, not on `main`). The number-cost report flags these daily (`NUMBER_WITHOUT_ENTITLEMENT_OUTSIDE_LIFECYCLE`). |
| 6 | **Orphan number receiving calls** | 1 number with no household in any environment; inbound calls as recently as 21 Sep. | LOW cost / HIGH investigation | Investigate before any release (never auto-release orphans). |
| 7 | **Per-household monitoring/AI and SMS have no daily or monthly ceiling on `main`** | `main` has the 30-min per-call monitoring cap and the concurrent-stream cap; the daily/period £ ceilings and allowance exist only on the unmerged allowance branch. A flood of long unknown calls, or many high-risk calls, costs streams, transcription and SMS without a daily bound. | MEDIUM | Allowance/financial-safety workstream (unmerged). Pricing decisions are separate. |
| 8 | **Unmonitored (trusted) minutes have no total bound** | Unconditional forwarding bills every call; only the 4-simultaneous-call limit (allowance branch) bounds the rate. | MEDIUM (commercial) | **DECISION REQUIRED:** the circuit-breaker design (L0–L4) awaits approval; the carrier comparison may change the economics. |
| 9 | **App leg could start being billed** | Twilio list price $0.004/min; billed £0 to date. That would cut £4.99 break-even from about 264 to 194 minutes (Google Play). | MEDIUM (commercial) | Detected automatically by the ledger (`charge_appeared_after_not_observed`, `supplier_charging_category_ledger_saw_as_uncharged`). |
| 10 | **Transcription cost can't be reconciled** | The OpenAI project key can't read costs; the ledger records USD estimates as `unreconciled`. No OpenAI spend cap visible from HCG. | MEDIUM | **DECISION REQUIRED:** create an OpenAI Admin key (read-only usage) and set an OpenAI monthly budget/limit in the OpenAI console. |
| 11 | **Number provisioning** | Single purchase path; at most 5 attempts per household; a race duplicate is released immediately; the RevenueCat webhook is authenticated; the Stripe webhook is verified. | Controlled | None. |
| 12 | **Outbound calling** | No outbound PSTN dialling in the current architecture (the old dial-back design cost £0.922, all before 23 Aug). | Controlled | None. The ledger would surface any `outbound_voice` cost immediately. |

**Automated daily controls available now** (built on the ledger branch, not deployed):
- `scripts/number-cost-report.js`: numbers, cost, anomalies, potential saving.
- `scripts/ledger-backfill.js` dry run: supplier vs ledger reconciliation and unallocated breakdown.
- `services/ledger/reconciliationWorker.js` `dailyReconciliation`: alerts on mismatches, newly charged categories and unmapped supplier categories.
