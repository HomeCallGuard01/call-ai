Delete your Home Call Guard account

Last updated: 25 August 2026

Home Call Guard is a trading name of AFMD Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales under company number 07075723. This page explains how to request deletion of your Home Call Guard account and the personal data associated with it.

How to request deletion

To delete your account, email us at support@homecallguard.co.uk from the email address associated with your Home Call Guard account, and let us know that you want your Home Call Guard account and associated personal data deleted.

We may need to verify your identity before completing the request, to make sure we don't delete someone else's account by mistake. We aim to complete deletion requests within 30 days.

What gets deleted

Once a deletion request is completed, we remove or anonymise:

  • Your account information — your email address, phone number, and other account details are removed or anonymised so the account can no longer be linked back to you.
  • Your trusted-contact information — the names and phone numbers you added to your trusted-contact list are deleted.
  • Your call and screening records — the history of screened calls associated with your account is deleted.

What we may need to keep

Some records may need to be retained even after your account is deleted, where we have a legal obligation to do so — for example, billing, transaction and accounting records that UK tax and accounting law requires us to keep. Where this applies, we keep only what the law requires, for only as long as the law requires it, and nothing beyond that.

Cancelling vs. deleting your account

Cancelling your subscription and deleting your account are different actions. Cancelling stops future billing and lets your protection continue until the end of the period you've already paid for — your account and data are not removed. Deleting your account, as described on this page, is a separate, additional step that removes your account, trusted-contact, and call history data as described above.

More information

For full detail on what personal data we collect, how we use it, and your rights, see our Privacy Policy.
