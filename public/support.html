Reporting a problem

If something isn't working — a call didn't ring through, forwarding won't activate, or the app is behaving unexpectedly — email us at the address above with:

  • A short description of what happened and what you expected instead
  • The approximate time it happened
  • Your account email address, so we can look up your household

Please don't include your password or full card details in an email — we'll never ask for either.

Common questions

How do I set up call forwarding?

Open the app and follow the Setup steps — they generate the exact code for your device and provider (mobile, or a UK landline provider such as BT, Sky, Virgin or TalkTalk) and confirm forwarding is active once you've dialled it.

How do trusted contacts work?

Anyone you add as a trusted contact always rings straight through, never screened. Add them from the Contacts tab in the app at any time.

How do I cancel or manage my membership?

Open Account → Membership in the app to manage your subscription. Cancelling stops future billing; your protection continues until the end of the period you've already paid for.

How do I delete my account?

See our account deletion page for how to request this and exactly what gets removed.

Is my data private?

Yes — see our Privacy Policy for full detail on what we collect and how it's used.
