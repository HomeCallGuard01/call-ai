Why it's different

Protection that goes beyond blocking known numbers.

Scammers can change or spoof the number they're calling from — so a call from an unfamiliar or unexpected number isn't automatically a warning sign, and a familiar-looking number isn't automatically safe.

Number-based blocking

Checks the number that's calling against lists of known risks — useful, and worth keeping.

+

Home Call Guard

Also looks for scam risk as the conversation itself develops — while you're talking, not just at the moment the phone rings.

Both matter. Home Call Guard is an extra layer on top of number-based protection, not a replacement for it.

How Home Call Guard works

Three things happen on a call.

Protection happens during the conversation, not before your phone rings. Home Call Guard doesn't block or delay a call before it reaches your phone.

  1. 1

    A trusted contact calls

    It rings straight through.

    Calls from the people you've added to your trusted list aren't monitored. Add family, friends or anyone else any time, from the app or your online account.

    Shown in Activity as
    Rang straight through

  2. 2

    Anyone else calls

    It connects as normal — and is monitored.

    Calls from numbers that aren't on your trusted list still ring your phone. While you talk, Home Call Guard monitors the conversation for signs of a scam.

    Shown in Activity as
    Screened, no concerns

  3. 3

    Serious scam risk is detected

    Home Call Guard can end the call.

    If the system detects serious scam risk during a call, it can end the call to help protect you.

    Shown in Activity as
    High risk — call stopped

Good to know. Home Call Guard can't promise to catch every scam, so please stay cautious with any unexpected call. We don't store call audio or a transcript of what's said. You can see how every call was handled in your Activity list at any time.

Questions

Everything you need to know.

Which phones and networks are supported?

Home Call Guard is available now for Android phones, on many UK mobile networks — we check your network before you pay, so you'll know before you subscribe. iPhone and landline support are in development and aren't available to new customers yet.

Are my calls recorded?

Home Call Guard doesn't store call audio or a transcript of what's said. Calls are analysed while they happen, using automated checks and a third-party transcription service. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Can I keep my phone number?

Yes. Your number stays exactly as it is.

How long does setup take?

There's no fixed length of time — we guide you through each step, from choosing your phone to adding trusted contacts and turning on call forwarding, so you always know what to do next.

How much does it cost, and can I cancel?

£4.99 a month, including VAT. There's no long-term contract and you can cancel at any time from your account. See our Terms and Conditions for full details.

Who can I contact for help?

Visit our support page or email support@homecallguard.co.uk and we'll be glad to help — including if you're setting this up for someone else.
