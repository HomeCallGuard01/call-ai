Questions

Everything you need to know.

Which phones and networks are supported?

Home Call Guard is available now for Android phones, on many UK mobile networks — we check your network before you pay, so you'll know before you subscribe. iPhone is coming soon and landline support is in development; neither is available to new customers yet.

How is this different from a call-blocking or caller-ID app?

Those apps decide based on the number that's calling, before you answer. That's useful, but a scammer can use a number that has never been reported, or fake a genuine one such as your bank's. Home Call Guard looks at what happens during the call — for example, a caller asking you to move money, read out a security code or install software on your device.

Will it stop every scam call?

No service can promise that, so it's still sensible to be careful with any unexpected call that asks for money, bank details or passwords.

Are my calls recorded?

No, Home Call Guard doesn't record your calls. When someone who isn't on your trusted list calls, the conversation is transcribed automatically while it happens, using a third-party service, so it can be checked for signs of a scam. We don't keep that transcript with your account — we keep a short record of each call, such as the time, the number and how it was handled. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Does one subscription cover more than one phone?

No. Each £4.99 subscription protects one phone number. To protect another phone, it needs its own subscription.

Can I cancel at any time?

Yes. There's no long-term contract. Cancelling stops future payments, and your protection continues until the end of the period you've already paid for. See our Terms and Conditions for full details.

Who can I contact for help?

Visit our support page or email support@homecallguard.co.uk and we'll be glad to help.
