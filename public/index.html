The problem

Scam calls don't always sound like scams.

Scam callers can sound polite, professional and convincing. They create urgency and pretend to be organisations you trust. It can happen to anyone.

Your bank

Claiming there's a problem with your account.

A delivery company

Asking for a payment or personal details.

Your energy supplier

Offering a refund or threatening disconnection.

A government department

Making an urgent or alarming claim.

Blocking a number only helps once that number is known, and scammers change and disguise the numbers they call from. Home Call Guard adds another layer: protection during the call itself. Read our scam advice guides →

How Home Call Guard works

Three things happen on a call.

Protection happens during the conversation, not before your phone rings. Home Call Guard doesn't block or delay a call before it reaches your phone.

Questions

Everything you need to know.

Does it stop scam calls before my phone rings?

No. Calls connect as normal and your phone rings. Home Call Guard works during the conversation: it monitors calls from numbers you don't know and can end a call if it detects serious scam risk. It can't promise to catch every scam, so please stay cautious with any unexpected call.

What does "monitoring" mean?

While you're on a call with someone who isn't a trusted contact, Home Call Guard monitors the conversation for signs of a scam.

What happens if it detects a scam call?

If Home Call Guard detects serious scam risk, it can end the call to help protect you. You can see how each call was handled in your Activity list.

Will calls from my family and friends be affected?

No. Trusted contacts ring straight through and aren't monitored.

Are my calls recorded?

Home Call Guard doesn't store call audio or a transcript of what's said. Calls are analysed while they happen, using automated checks and a third-party transcription service. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Do I need to install an app?

To protect an Android phone you use the Home Call Guard app from Google Play. You can also manage your account, trusted contacts and Activity from your online account. iPhone and landline protection are coming soon.

Can I use Home Call Guard on my landline?

Not yet. Landline protection is coming soon and we aren't selling it today. Home Call Guard is currently available for Android phones.

Which phones and networks are supported?

Home Call Guard is available for Android phones on many UK mobile networks. We check your network before you pay, so you'll know before you subscribe. iPhone and landline support are coming soon.

Can I keep my phone number?

Yes. Your number stays exactly as it is.

How long does setup take?

Usually a few minutes: sign up, add your trusted contacts, then dial a one-time code. We show you the exact steps.

How much does it cost, and can I cancel?

£4.99 a month, including VAT. There's no long-term contract and you can cancel at any time from your account. See our Terms and Conditions for full details.

Who can I contact for help?

Visit our support page or email support@homecallguard.co.uk and we'll be glad to help — including if you're setting this up for someone else.
